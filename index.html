Why prepare?

In Week 9 you play the Portfolio Checklist Game. You check your portfolio against the assessment criteria. You need to:

  • Have your files organised and easy to open
  • Know where all your work is saved
  • Have read the criteria
  • Be ready to improve your work in class

Make a main folder called Portfolio_Term2. Inside, make these folders:

  • 📁 Introduction
  • 📁 Research_Pages
  • 📁 Design_Development
  • 📁 Refinement_Selection
  • 📁 Final_Lineup
  • 📁 Bibliography
  • Move all your files into the correct folders
  • Name files clearly, e.g. Research_Page_01.psd

Your portfolio should include:

  • Introduction page – project title and context
  • Research pages – different sources and your own responses
  • Design development pages – 2D and 3D ideas with research links
  • Refinement & Selection – 1 page of similar but different design ideas, to select, edit and inform your final lineup
  • Colour/fabric palette page – with research links
  • Final lineup / outcomes

Missing a page? Start it now!

For every image you need:

  • Artist or creator's name
  • Year (if known)
  • Title of the work
  • Where you found it (website, book, museum)
📚 Harvard format

Artist's Last Name, First Initial. (Year). Title of the work [Image].

Example: Turner, L. (2018). Abstract concepts in digital art [Image].

Make a simple list of all your sources. You need it in class!

Harvard Referencing Guide ↗

Your portfolio is assessed on:

  • Subheadings & Layout – clear, creative, with graphic elements
  • Research & Inspiration – different sources with your responses
  • Design Development – 2D and 3D ideas with research links
  • Refinement & Selection – 1 page of similar but different ideas
  • Referencing – sources properly credited
  • Colour Palette – relevant, with research support
  • Typography – consistent and appropriate
  • Visual Elements – high quality, supporting your message

Which areas are your strengths? Which need improvement?

Ask yourself:

  • Does my introduction clearly explain my project?
  • Can I see a clear link between research and design?
  • Have I shown my thinking and decisions?
  • Are my subheadings helpful and creative?
  • Do I have enough different research sources?
  • Does my work look professional and organised?
💡 Tip

Make notes about anything you're unsure about. You can work on these in Week 9.

  • Laptop fully charged
  • Photoshop installed and working
  • You can open all your files (not on a drive you might forget!)
  • Good internet connection and a working microphone
  • You know how to export a JPEG: File › Export › Export As… › Format: JPG
